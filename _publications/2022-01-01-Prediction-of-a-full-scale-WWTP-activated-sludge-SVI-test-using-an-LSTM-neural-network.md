## Abstract

Activated sludge settling properties, typically characterized by the Sludge Volume Index (SVI), are critical for the operational stability of secondary clarifiers in biological wastewater treatment plants. Unfavorable settling conditions can induce sludge bulking, leading to severe operational disruptions and effluent standard violations.

This paper presents an LSTM-based deep neural network that accurately anticipates SVI fluctuations several days in advance, providing operators with predictive capability to adjust chemical dosing and aeration prior to settling deterioration.
