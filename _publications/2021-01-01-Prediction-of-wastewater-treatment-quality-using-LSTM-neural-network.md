## Overview

Predicting effluent quality parameters in wastewater treatment plants helps with process monitoring and compliance. In this paper, we evaluate LSTM networks for predicting water quality parameters (such as Chemical Oxygen Demand and Total Suspended Solids) using operational sensor measurements from a treatment facility.
